Presentation Strategy for Simply Embedded

Prepared for: David & Tharinda
Objective: Position Telemetra as the software platform that complements Simply Embedded’s hardware and firmware expertise.

⸻

1. Background

About Simply Embedded

Simply Embedded is a Calgary-based embedded systems engineering company specializing in designing and building custom connected devices for industrial customers.

Their expertise appears to include:

* Custom electronics design
* PCB design
* Firmware development
* Embedded Linux
* Industrial gateways
* LTE communications
* Satellite failover communications
* Device manufacturing
* Industrial automation
* SCADA integration
* Cloud connectivity
* Product development for OEM customers

Their value proposition is primarily on the edge of the IoT ecosystem.

They build devices that reliably collect and transmit data.

⸻

2. Our Assumption

Based on their website and our conversation, it appears they do not position themselves as a configurable IoT platform provider.

They likely deliver custom projects where software is built on a per-customer basis or integrated into existing customer systems.

That creates an opportunity.

⸻

3. The Gap

Simply Embedded is excellent at getting data from machines.

But industrial customers eventually need much more than connectivity.

Eventually every customer asks for:

* Dashboards
* Reports
* Historical analytics
* Alarm notifications
* User management
* Device management
* Firmware management
* Customer portals
* Multi-site management
* Mobile access
* APIs
* AI insights
* Digital twins

These are software products rather than hardware products.

Building these repeatedly is expensive.

⸻

4. Our Positioning

This is probably the most important message.

We are NOT another hardware company.

We are NOT competing with their firmware team.

We are NOT replacing SCADA.

Instead…

Telemetra is the software operating system that sits above the hardware.

⸻

Instead of saying:

We built an IoT platform.

We should say:

You already build excellent connected hardware. We provide the industrial software layer your customers expect.

That changes the conversation from competition to partnership.

14. Recommendations for the Codex Demo

Rather than building a generic IoT dashboard, tailor the UI to Simply Embedded’s world. Mock up screens such as:

* Fleet Overview showing gateways with LTE/satellite status on a map.
* Gateway Details with signal strength, firmware version, uptime, and remote configuration.
* SCADA Integration View displaying tags flowing from PLCs into Telemetra.
* Remote Site Dashboard for a pump station or industrial facility with live telemetry, alarms, and historical trends.
* AI Operations Assistant where a user asks, “Why did Site 4 go offline?” and receives a contextual explanation.
* Customer Portal demonstrating how different end customers each have isolated data and branding.

These screens will help Simply Embedded visualize how their hardware could become part of a complete industrial software offering.

⸻

Key Message

The presentation should consistently reinforce one central idea:

Simply Embedded builds the connected industrial hardware. Telemetra transforms that hardware into a complete, modern industrial software solution that customers can operate, monitor, analyze, and scale.

If the discussion stays focused on that partnership narrative instead of individual features, you’ll be speaking directly to their business rather than just showcasing another IoT platform.
